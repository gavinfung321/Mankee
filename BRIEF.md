# Mankee Website Rebuild Brief

Status: Creative direction selected for questions 1–7. Mankee has authorised reuse of its controlled website and social assets. The connected Fuk Wing Street shopfront structure and operating hours are owner-confirmed; remaining operational details still require verification.

Language decision: Traditional Chinese for Hong Kong (`zh-HK`) is the primary authoring and interface language. English is supporting operational content only unless a separate English version is approved later.

This document follows the ScrollCraft briefing format. Statements are labelled as user direction, verified evidence, working proposal, or open decision. Working proposals must not be treated as owner-approved facts.

## 1. What this is and who it is for

**User direction**

Rebuild the website for 文記車仔麵 / Man Kee Cart Noodles, a popular Hong Kong cart-noodle restaurant, as a modern yet classic scroll-driven experience with 3D animation.

The owner context is that Sham Shui Po is the main store and the success of Mankee begins there. The Tsim Sha Tsui mall location is operated as a collaboration with other owners. The purpose is to bring back the old Hong Kong cart-noodle style and continue that tradition.

**Verified evidence**

- Mankee's main store in Sham Shui Po spans four connected Fuk Wing Street shopfronts at 109, 115, 121 and 123C rather than four separate branches.
- Tsim Sha Tsui is a collaboration location inside a mall and is not the origin or main brand story.
- The business presents a traditional ordering experience alongside newer technology and conveyor-belt service.
- Current public channels serve both Chinese- and English-speaking visitors.
- The official Instagram also references a Tsim Sha Tsui location and online delivery.

**Audience hypothesis, pending approval**

1. Hong Kong diners choosing a branch or ordering food.
2. Visitors seeking an iconic local-food experience.
3. Media and partners checking the brand story and recognition.

## 2. What the visitor must believe by the end

**Owner-supported narrative direction**

Mankee brings the old Hong Kong cart-noodle style back to life in Sham Shui Po and carries that tradition forward.

## 3. Primary visitor action

**Owner-approved navigation decision**

The main navigation contains one highlighted **分店** item. There is no separate global “搵間文記” action because it duplicates the same destination.

Inside the location section, visitors choose a branch and then receive specific actions: 店舖資料, 地圖導航 and 外賣落單. Menu access remains a normal primary navigation item.

## 4. Vibe and references

**User direction**

- Modern and classic.
- Premium 3D animation.
- Rooted in a very popular Hong Kong cart-noodle restaurant.

**Owner-selected direction: Hong Kong night market, refined**

- Authentic, cinematic, energetic, tactile and confident.
- Sham Shui Po after dark, contemporary Hong Kong editorial photography and Wong Kar-wai lighting are reference territory, not designs to copy.
- Authentic Sham Shui Po energy rather than generic luxury dining.
- Lacquer red, deep charcoal, warm yellow, stainless steel and controlled blue light.
- Traditional vertical ingredient strips, dense food texture and Hong Kong street signage.
- Modern precision appears through lighting, motion and the conveyor system.
- Avoid cream-and-gold heritage styling, low-poly food, generic neon cyberpunk and invented nostalgia.

Reference media should come from culture beyond websites where possible. Owner references are still required: up to three films, restaurants, magazines, album covers, shops or physical spaces.

## 5. Proposed scroll journey

**Owner-selected journey: Heritage to continuation**

1. **One Hong Kong bowl**: a split opening presents old Hong Kong cart-noodle culture and Mankee carrying it forward today.
2. **Food takes over**: signature toppings, texture and the appetite of a real Mankee bowl.
3. **Build your bowl**: noodles, soup bases, toppings and vegetables become a collection.
4. **文記十選**: verified recommendations and signature combinations.
5. **The tradition continues**: the visual language of old Hong Kong moves into the present without losing its character.
6. **Recognised and loved**: Michelin, Tourism Board and selected media coverage.
7. **分店**: location-specific directions, hours and delivery.

## 6. Energy and feeling curve

**Owner-selected energy: Slow build to one major peak**

The opening is calm, the history is warm and nostalgic, ingredient selection introduces movement, a quiet pause precedes the large old-meets-new animation, and the ending returns to calm confidence.

| Beat | Intended feeling | On-screen cause |
|---|---|---|
| One Hong Kong bowl | Recognition | Mankee red, logo, real food, old Hong Kong cart-noodle culture and present-day Sham Shui Po appear together; Traditional Chinese carries the headline |
| Food takes over | Appetite | Signature toppings and broth texture fill the frame |
| Build your bowl | Curiosity | Ingredients separate into a precise, labelled collection |
| The Mankee Ten | Appetite | Real toppings approach and assemble around the bowl |
| The tradition continues | Delight | The central noodle strand crosses from the old Hong Kong side into present-day Mankee and pulls both into one bowl |
| Recognised and loved | Confidence | Verified recognition and real third-party coverage replace advertising claims |
| 分店 | Resolve | The selected branch becomes a clear route, schedule and order path |

## 7. Peak and memorable moment

**Owner-selected peak: The noodle divide**

Peak sentence a visitor would say to a friend:

> The noodle strand divided old and new Hong Kong, then pulled the ingredients together into one Mankee bowl.

Peak location: The tradition continues.

The act before the peak should be quieter and more orderly. The peak receives the best asset work and the most scroll room. The ending must resolve into an actionable location choice rather than fade into a footer.

## 8. Signature move

**Owner-selected useful behaviour: Bowl memory**

The split between traditional and modern Mankee is drawn by a single noodle strand. Scroll tension bends the strand; verified ingredient cutouts travel along it; both sides collapse into a completed bowl. The site remembers the ingredients the visitor passed or selected and presents that bowl again at the ending. A location choice made later retains the visitor's chosen context through the final action.

This must work as a complete static composition on reduced-motion devices. Desktop pointer movement may add subtle depth but cannot carry meaning.

## 9. Structural choice

**Owner-selected structure, refined by owner context: Origin versus continuation Split Stage**

ScrollCraft grammar: **Split Stage**.

Why it fits: the brand has a real two-sided story, the old Hong Kong cart-noodle culture it values and the way Mankee continues that tradition today. Both sides are anchored in the Sham Shui Po main store. The split is not a comparison between Sham Shui Po and Tsim Sha Tsui, and Tsim Sha Tsui does not represent the modern side.

Why a continuous world is not proposed: the story is conceptual rather than geographical, and a seamless generated flight would be expensive, fragile and less authentic.

Why a filmic one-shot is not proposed: it would turn a specific contrast into a generic promotional film.

Why a gallery/catalog is secondary: the menu range suits a collection, but the brand-level homepage needs the old/new relationship to lead. A separate menu page may use catalog behaviour without changing the homepage grammar.
