# Mankee Website Review Checklist

Status: Approved by owner on 7 October 2026. Wireframing may begin.

## Already confirmed

- Primary language: Traditional Chinese for Hong Kong.
- Main navigation: 首頁、菜單、文記故事、分店.
- Main story: bring back the old Hong Kong cart-noodle style and continue the tradition.
- Narrative centre: Sham Shui Po main store and Fuk Wing Street.
- Sham Shui Po structure: one location across four connected shopfronts.
- Sham Shui Po hours: daily 11:00–04:00.
- Tsim Sha Tsui role: supporting mall collaboration location.
- Public Tsim Sha Tsui label: 尖沙咀店.
- Public Sham Shui Po label: 深水埗店.
- Provisional 3D bowl ingredients: 上湯花膠、玻璃辣魷魚、瑞士雞中翼.
- ScrollCraft grammar under review: Concept B chapter-led composition with a shared 3D bowl, edge noodle memory and nine individually scored device families.
- Emotional peak: the noodle divider pulls remembered ingredients into one Mankee bowl.
- Ending: Sham Shui Po resolves first, with 尖沙咀店 available secondarily.
- Public narrative boundary: food is the main subject. “Success began in Sham Shui Po” is owner-supplied planning context and must not appear in audience copy.
- Recognition priority: the documented visit by 《黑白大廚：料理階級大戰》 judge 白種元 is a major proof point and should receive more prominence than a generic media card.
- Founder and visit stories: 黃志強 receives a dedicated cultural-purpose chapter; 黎芷珊 appears as a documented store visit using the current homepage as the wording and photo reference.
- Motion coverage: every homepage chapter must name its ScrollCraft device, explain its handoff and avoid repeating the preceding device family.

## Review now

Review only these three parts of the storyboard:

1. **Opening**: Old Hong Kong cart-noodle culture appears on one side and present-day Sham Shui Po Mankee on the other.
2. **Peak**: The noodle divider becomes three-dimensional and carries ingredients into one accurate Mankee bowl.
3. **Ending**: 深水埗 appears first with directions, hours and delivery; 尖沙咀店 remains available as a secondary choice.

If these three moments tell the correct brand story, the structure is ready for wireframes.
