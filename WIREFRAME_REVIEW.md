# Mankee homepage wireframe review

Status: Concept B revised with nine individually scored ScrollCraft chapters, founder story, documented 白種元 and 黎芷珊 visits, and visible transition contracts. Awaiting owner direction.

## What the noodle means

The noodle is a timeline of flavour and continuity. It begins with old Hong Kong cart-noodle culture, passes through signature toppings, personal selection and preparation, then becomes a physical noodle carrying remembered ingredients into one Mankee bowl.

It does not represent the boundary between the Sham Shui Po and Tsim Sha Tsui stores. Food is the public-facing subject. Sham Shui Po is the principal photographic setting and the first location shown at the practical ending. Tsim Sha Tsui appears only in that final location section as a secondary collaboration store.

The owner's statement that Mankee's success began in Sham Shui Po is internal planning context, not audience-facing copy. Do not publish that phrase or turn the homepage into a business-success story.

## Concept A: persistent noodle thread

- Desktop: a vertical noodle divides the origin and continuation halves through most of the story.
- Mobile: the noodle becomes horizontal and the two halves stack above and below it.
- Strength: the final 3D transformation is strongly foreshadowed and the site has a distinctive structural identity.
- Risk: visitors may interpret the halves as two shops, or the repeated split may constrain photography.
- Mitigation: use explicit origin/continuation labels, avoid showing Tsim Sha Tsui before the location ending and allow selected acts to break the split.

## Concept B: chapter noodles

- Each chapter uses the composition best suited to its content.
- Short noodle marks accumulate at important transitions.
- Immediately before the peak, those marks connect into one physical noodle.
- The shared 3D bowl opens the site, hands off into an ingredient macro and returns for the assembly peak.
- A fixed edge rail names every transition and shows remembered ingredients accumulating during this wireframe review.
- Every chapter names its own ScrollCraft device. Adjacent chapters do not repeat a device family.
- Strength: clearer editorial reading and more freedom for food photography.
- Risk: the signature move has less continuous setup and may feel added late.
- Mitigation: maintain three unmistakable noodle-memory moments before the peak.

## Recommendation

Choose Concept B if brand clarity and photographic flexibility are the priorities. Choose Concept A if Mankee wants the website to feel more experimental and is comfortable with a strong conceptual device.

The current recommendation is the revised Concept B: a scroll-responsive 3D hero, flexible photographic chapters and a subtle noodle progress indicator at the page edge. The same bowl is reused at the assembly peak, so the opening animation pays off later instead of acting as decoration.

## Approval questions

1. Is the first screen clearly Mankee, with food unmistakably the main subject?
2. Does the journey from cart-noodle tradition to signature toppings, self-selection and the completed bowl feel natural?
3. Does revised Concept B now make the connection between chapters and the return of the Hero bowl understandable?
4. Is the closing hierarchy correct: Sham Shui Po first, Tsim Sha Tsui secondary?
5. Is the 白種元 visit prominent enough in the recognition chapter?
6. Does the founder story explain cultural purpose without becoming a success story?
7. Is the 黎芷珊 visit represented with the right prominence and factual restraint?

Typography, final colour, final crop, animation timing, 3D accuracy and finished copy are intentionally outside this approval round.
