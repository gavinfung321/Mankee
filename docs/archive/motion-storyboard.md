# Mankee Scroll-Animation Storyboard

Status: Food-first revision aligned to owner feedback. Homepage grammar remains under wireframe review.

Grammar under review: persistent Split Stage (Concept A) versus chapter-led composition with restrained noodle markers (Concept B).

World: Refined Hong Kong night market using real Mankee photography, warm tungsten, cool stainless-steel fill, lacquer red, menu-board yellow, charcoal and restrained film grain.

Signature move: Bowl Memory. Ingredients encountered or deliberately selected by the visitor are remembered by the noodle motif, travel into the peak and settle into one completed bowl. The selected context remains visible at the final location action.

## 1. Structural rules

- Food is the public-facing subject. Sham Shui Po supplies the principal real-world setting, not a public story about where business success began.
- The statement that success began in Sham Shui Po is internal owner context and must not appear in audience copy.
- Tsim Sha Tsui is not one side of any split and appears only as the secondary option in the practical location ending.
- The noodle may be a persistent divider or restrained chapter marker pending wireframe selection.
- No horizontal pan rail, spotlight or magnetic button.
- No more than one scrub video; the current plan uses none unless real footage later clearly earns it.
- One shared 3D bowl scene appears in the hero and returns for the noodle-and-bowl peak. Do not load two independent renderers.
- At least four motion families are used, with no family repeated in adjacent beats.
- The opening is already complete and readable before scrolling.

## 3. Scene-by-scene storyboard

### Act 1: 一碗香港

Purpose: Establish the entire idea in the first screen.

Desktop composition:

- Left half: the visual language of old Hong Kong cart noodles, expressed through real bowls, menu strips and food-service texture.
- Right half: a present-day Mankee bowl and preparation detail showing the same food tradition continuing today.
- Centre: one noodle strand forms the divider. The Mankee mark sits at its top anchor.
- Headline is split across both sides, but reads as one sentence: 「一碗香港，兩代相傳」.
- Supporting line: 「由麵底、湯底到餸菜，自由配搭出你的一碗。」 This remains draft copy until owner approval.

Motion family: restrained parallax plus one kinetic line treatment.

Hero depth and 3D contract:

- The opening bowl is a real 3D or accurately reconstructed Mankee bowl at a restrained 15-degree tilt, with independent bowl, ingredient, noodle, steam and background planes.
- Initial scrolling rotates the bowl toward the camera and slightly separates 上湯花膠、玻璃辣魷魚 and 瑞士雞中翼 to establish depth.
- The camera finishes by pushing into one topping macro, which becomes the opening frame of Act 2.
- The same bowl object returns in Act 6. This makes the hero and peak one story rather than two unrelated effects.

Scroll choreography:

1. Landing frame holds at 50/50 with all essential copy readable.
2. Far environments move 20–30 px in opposing directions.
3. Historical counter details and present-day Sham Shui Po service layers move 45–70 px, creating real depth.
4. The noodle divider responds with slight tension, not a wave or bounce.
5. The headline finishes assembling before the act exits.

Required assets:

- Clean traditional and modern environment plates.
- Separate foreground counter/conveyor layers.
- Logo master.
- Static desktop and phone posters.

Fallback: One static split poster with semantic headline and navigation.

### Act 2: 碗內主角

Left side:

Right side:

- A wider Mankee bowl and serving detail connect the food macro to a real meal.
- Short copy leads naturally into the cart-noodle selection system in Act 3.

Motion family: full-height directional reveal.

Scroll choreography:

2. The complete bowl reveals from the opposite side.
3. Three ingredient names become the first remembered food markers.
4. Both food scales finish visible together before the selection system begins.

Fallback: Asymmetric two-column food editorial section.

Right side:

4. The three selections become the remembered ingredients that lead into 本店十大必食.
5. A plain link leads to the complete 菜單 page.

Composition:

### Act 6: 靜候一刻

Purpose: Create authored silence before the peak.

Composition:

- Both halves simplify to their material surfaces: warm counter wood on the left, brushed stainless steel on the right.
- Food objects stop moving.
- Copy is limited to one quiet line or omitted after user testing.
- Steam crosses the divider very slowly.

Motion family: unpinned ground-only flow with minimal atmospheric opacity.

Scroll choreography:

1. Menu elements leave the frame.
2. The ingredient knots remain on the divider.
3. The noodle strand tightens by a few pixels.
4. The next act begins before the pause feels like a loading state.

Fallback: A short separator with no surplus viewport height.

### Act 7: 一條麵，連起兩代

Purpose: Deliver the one moment visitors remember.

Peak sentence:

> The noodle strand divided old and new Hong Kong, then pulled the ingredients together into one Mankee bowl.

Motion family: custom Three.js bowl and noodle system inside a pinned Split Stage.

Scroll choreography:

1. The flat divider gains volume and becomes a physical noodle strand.
2. Its centre bends toward the viewer while both endpoints remain anchored.
3. Remembered ingredient tokens convert into real cutouts or lightweight geometry.
4. Ingredients travel along the noodle at different intervals, never floating without a path.
5. An accurately modelled Mankee bowl rises from the bottom centre with its contact shadow attached. The provisional hero combination is 上湯花膠、玻璃辣魷魚、瑞士雞中翼.
6. The ingredients settle into the bowl without morphing or changing identity.
7. Old Hong Kong and present-day Sham Shui Po backgrounds align behind the completed bowl.
8. The line 「兩代方法，一碗文記」 appears only after the assembly lands.

3D boundaries:

- Model the bowl, plate, noodle curve and only the few ingredients needed for the final composition.
- Use the supplied Mankee bowl close-up as the primary material, colour, bowl-pattern and plating-density reference.
- Before accurate modelling, obtain accurate references for 上湯花膠、玻璃辣魷魚 and 瑞士雞中翼, then capture the combined serving from full overhead and unobstructed 30-degree angles.
- The supplied PackTravel watermarked bowl may guide overall bowl shape, camera angle, soup depth and plating density only. Do not reproduce or publish the image, and do not assume its visible toppings are the approved hero combination.
- Use the new near-overhead reference to plan ingredient zones, the noodle-lift reference to choreograph the strand leaving the bowl and the complete low-angle reference to establish the final silhouette and contact shadow. These guide staging only until their rights are confirmed.
- Use photographic alpha cards for ingredients when geometry adds no value.
- Avoid simulated soup, complex fluid dynamics or a full 3D restaurant.
- Keep the final bowl accurate to a real approved serving.

Performance target:

- Load near the preceding act.
- Core GLB approximately 3–5 MB or less.
- Cap device pixel ratio on phones.
- Pause requestAnimationFrame when offscreen.
- Provide an exact final-scene poster for no-WebGL and reduced-motion modes.

## 4. Bowl Memory behavior

The interaction records only temporary page state:

```text
encounter dish -> add visual knot to divider
select dish -> strengthen its knot and store up to three selections
enter peak -> convert stored knots into travelling ingredients
complete peak -> settle them in the bowl composition
select location -> retain a small bowl echo beside the action panel
leave session -> state may reset
```

Rules:

- It is not a shopping basket.
- It never promises that the exact visual combination can be ordered as shown.
- It works with scroll alone; pointer selection is an enhancement.
- Keyboard selection produces the same state.
- Reduced motion keeps the chosen names and final static bowl without travel animation.

## 5. Desktop layer contract

| Plane | Planned asset | Movement | Contact and occlusion rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Far left | Traditional shop clean plate | Small warm lateral shift | No duplicate bowl, person or sign behind cutouts |
| Far right | Modern conveyor clean plate | Small cool lateral shift | Perspective aligns with the central stage |
| Mid left | Menu strips and counter detail | Moderate depth shift | Anchored to the traditional plate |
| Mid right | Conveyor trays and stainless detail | Moderate opposing shift | No invented machinery or floating controls |
| Focal | Accurate Mankee bowl and plate | Deliberate rise and restrained rotation | Bowl, plate and contact shadow move together |
| Divider | Noodle curve/mesh | Tension, bend and final collapse | Endpoints remain visually anchored until resolve |
| Near | Ingredient cutouts and counter edges | Strongest restrained shift | Preserve realistic scale and essential copy |
| Atmosphere | Real steam and controlled haze | Slow opacity and drift | Never wash out food colour or text |
| Type/UI | Semantic HTML | Stable or carefully staged | Always readable and keyboard accessible |

## 6. Mobile composition

Mobile is not a scaled desktop scene.

- The split becomes top/bottom with a horizontal noodle divider.
- Both worlds remain visible in the opening: traditional above, modern below.
- Copy moves into dedicated high-contrast areas rather than sitting over busy food.
- Ingredient lists become tap-friendly rows, not tiny floating labels.
- The 3D peak uses fewer ingredients, a closer bowl crop and shorter travel.
- On lower-capability devices, the peak uses the exact rendered poster plus semantic ingredient names.
- The closing split gives each location a large half-screen target before expanding the selected one.
- Test at 390 × 844 and compact 360 × 640.

## 7. Reduced-motion composition

- Remove pinned travel that exists only for animation.
- Display the hero as a complete static split composition.
- Present the ten recommendations as an ordinary numbered list or grid.
- Replace travelling ingredients with an immediate selected-state change.
- Show the peak's final bowl poster with the headline already resolved.
- Keep location selection, maps and delivery links fully interactive.

Capture and inspect:

- The first and final frame of the 3D peak.
- Bowl-memory state with zero, one and three selected dishes.
