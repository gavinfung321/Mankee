## Audit of the current section

What the analysis rejects, confirmed in the live layout:

- Desktop preview is `520×635` inside a `16px` rounded frame. The section is `672px` tall, and the photograph is the dominant object.
- Mobile preview is also `520px` tall. Ten bordered rows follow it in one column. The section measures `1356px`, taller than a phone screen, so the list and the photograph compete.
- From `48rem` upward the names sit in two columns of bordered buttons, about `49px` tall, with a full lacquer fill on the active row (`#a51e25`). They read as settings controls, not a menu board.
- The preview uses `object-fit: cover` on a tall portrait frame, which is the crop the analysis describes as an abstract wet texture.
- Several rows can be opened independently. The red state is a filled button, not one restrained marker.
- `.top-ten-list` is also used on the menu page. New ledger rules must stay under `#top-ten` so that page is left alone.
- In the 2026-10-11 review recording, the full-width jade `HowToOrder` block followed by this full-width black block reads as two stacked colour bands. The colours are individually relevant to the brand, but their scale and alternation make the page feel dated.
- The landscape preview begins at the upper-right corner while the title and all interaction remain on the left. It reads as a detached thumbnail and leaves a large unused dark area below it.

The 2026-10-11 23:13 review identifies the remaining light-surface problems:

- Warm paper, pale rules, muted numbers and plain ink names have almost the same visual energy. The result is technically clean but visually flat.
- The unselected `□` is a Unicode font glyph, not a designed control. Its baseline, weight and proportions do not match the selected tick, so it looks accidental and can vary by platform.
- `已揀 6 款` is aligned to the far edge of the whole section, far away from both the instruction and the ledger it summarises.
- The preview photograph is now lower, but it remains a bare rectangle floating in blank paper. It has no small label or frame that connects it to the focused row.
- The current fish-meat crop reads as a pale close-up texture rather than a clearly recognisable dish. Image replacement remains a separate media task, but the preview container must still look intentional before new photographs arrive.

The 2026-10-12 review identifies one further hierarchy problem after the functional refinement:

- Kicker, title, subtitle, instruction, topping names and preview header mostly rely on the same dark-ink treatment. Size changes alone are not creating enough semantic separation.
- The plain two-digit numbers still look like ordinary list metadata rather than the organising system for a signature-ten ledger.
- The instruction/count line is better positioned but visually merges with the rows below it.
- The preview panel is understandable, but its pale header has almost the same value as the page and the panel begins several rows down, leaving an unproductive upper-right void.
- When many toppings are selected, repeating red `已揀` ten times creates a wall of red status text. The state must remain clear without competing with the ingredient names.

The follow-up 2026-10-12 review confirms that the semantic hierarchy pass resolves those problems:

- The pale-sage instruction rail, jade index tabs, ink names and restrained lacquer states are visually distinct.
- The `10 / 10` stress state remains readable and the ingredient names stay primary.
- The preview header now reads as part of the interface and begins level with the ledger rail.
- Do not reopen the layout, palette, index treatment or action-state design during the remaining image and motion work.

`OrderSlipFollow` already reads `#top-ten .top-ten-dish[open] summary` and expects the full dish name. This plan does not change that component. The ledger must keep that hook working.

## Recommended direction

Replace the button grid with ten numbered ledger rows and one controlled preview.

- Numbers `01–10` are small and tabular in deep jade on the light surface. Do not use muddy low-contrast gold for functional numbering.
- Dish names use the established Chinese display/serif face in strong ink, at a visibly larger size and weight than the instruction and actions.
- Rows are separated by thin rules. They are not cards, bordered buttons, or filled rectangles.
- More than one topping may be selected. Each selected row receives a consistent lacquer-red tick and `已揀`; red is never a solid row background fill.
- One row may be previewed at a time. Preview focus uses stronger ink type or a short jade marker, not the selected-state red marker.
- The preview becomes a square-edged editorial preview panel: one thin ink rule/frame, a small context label, and the height-capped photograph. No rounded card and no shadow.
- Replace the full-width ink ground with the established warm paper surface. Use ink for type, separators and contained UI only; do not make another full-screen dark band.
- Keep jade and lacquer as accents: jade may identify the chapter or preview state, while lacquer marks selected toppings. Neither becomes a large background fill.
- Make the heading block span the available width before the ledger/preview split. The photograph must begin with the interactive content, not float beside the heading in the upper-right corner.
- The preview must not repeat the active dish name as a large caption over the photograph; the active ledger row already supplies that information.
- No pin, no scroll-scrub, and no sequence of ten full-screen photographs.

## Desktop composition — 1440px

Reading order: full-width kicker and heading; then the ledger header and rows in the main left column, with the preview panel in the secondary right column.

- Use an asymmetric grid of approximately **60% ledger / 40% preview**. Do not give the image and ledger equal visual weight.
- Give the `文記十大` chapter approximately **72–80px desktop top padding** after `HowToOrder`. Separate the chapters with whitespace and one restrained rule, not a dark colour band, glow or gradient seam.
- The ledger is one column of ten rows on the left, not two columns of buttons.
- The preview sits on the right and begins level with the first ledger row, not level with the section title. Centre it vertically within the ledger zone when the available height allows. Use a landscape frame close to `4:3`, and do not grow past **280px** tall.
- The preview width follows its column. It does not span the section.
- Give the preview a small header such as `目前預覽  07 手打魚肉`, with the number in jade and the dish name in ink. This is interface context, not a second display heading.
- Put the instruction and count in one compact ledger header, constrained to the ledger column. Use `點選配料，可揀多款` on the left and `已揀 6 / 10` on the right. The changing number may use lacquer; the status must not sit at the far edge of the full section.
- Selected row: one designed lacquer-red tick with `已揀`. Multiple selected rows are allowed. Do not use a red panel.
- Preview row: stronger ink type or one short jade marker. Only one row is previewed at a time.
- Give the row a dedicated narrow number column, followed by the full dish name. Keep alignment exact across all ten rows.
- Separate rows with low-contrast ink hairlines. Do not draw an outer box around the ledger.
- Section padding stays in the current home-section scale. Target a section that is clearly shorter than a full `900px` viewport. Do not use `100vh`, sticky positioning, or a pinned stage.
- The disclaimer sits under the ledger, quiet, in the sans face.

## Interaction and state model

Maintain two independent values:

1. `previewId` — exactly one dish whose photograph is displayed. It begins at `01 上湯花膠`.
2. `selectedIds` — a set containing zero, one, or several toppings chosen for the future order slip. It begins empty.

The selected values remain available as full stable dish IDs/names in confirmed menu order. A later integration task will write these selected names into the 餸 area of the order slip. Do not edit `OrderSlipFollow` during the ledger multi-select pass.

Show one clear instruction near the ledger: `點選配料，可揀多款`. Show a compact live count such as `已揀 2 款` when selections exist. Do not add prices, quantities, a checkout button, or an order total.

### Selection discoverability refinement

The first multi-select implementation is functional but too subtle. Tiny isolated red squares look decorative, and the current instruction does not clearly say that each row is interactive.

- Increase the instruction and live count to a clearly readable small-body size; do not treat them as faint metadata.
- Give every row a dedicated right-hand action column.
- Do not render Unicode `□` or `☑` characters. They are inconsistent font glyphs, not controls.
- Unselected state: `＋ 加入` in a restrained deep-jade or muted-ink treatment.
- Selected state: a CSS-drawn or icon-system tick plus `已揀` in lacquer red. The tick and label must share a consistent baseline and visual weight.
- The entire row remains clickable/tappable; the label reinforces the action and is not a separate tiny target.
- On pointer hover or keyboard focus, use a short jade leading rule or a very light sage wash plus the pointer cursor. Do not turn the row into a bordered card or filled red button.
- Keep preview focus separate: one short jade line and slightly stronger ink name. Do not label preview focus as selected.
- Keep `已揀 N / 10` at the opposite end of the ledger-width instruction line and update it immediately.

### Light-surface hierarchy

- Background: warm paper, quiet and neutral.
- Section kicker and row numbers: deep jade with sufficient contrast.
- Main heading and topping names: strong ink.
- Instruction and dividers: muted ink, never pale grey.
- Selected state only: lacquer red.
- Preview panel: one thin ink frame/rule and a very pale sage context strip if needed. No beige card, shadow, radius or heavy black block.
- Rows should be approximately `50–54px` tall on desktop so the names feel intentional and the action column can breathe.
- Increase topping-name size modestly; keep it below the section heading and above all metadata.

### Colour and typographic hierarchy refinement

Use colour by semantic role rather than applying ink to every layer:

| Element | Treatment | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Short rule | Lacquer red | Brand punctuation only |
| Kicker `十大推介` | Deep jade, small sans | Chapter label |
| Heading `文記十大` | Strong near-black ink, Kai/display | Primary section identity |
| Subtitle `可以由這十款開始` | Muted graphite, smaller serif/sans | Supporting proposition |
| Instruction/count rail | Very pale sage strip, square edges | Functional interaction zone |
| Instruction | Strong ink | Explain what to do |
| Count | Muted ink label with lacquer-red number | Confirm selection without floating away |
| Row number | Ivory numerals inside a small square deep-jade tab | Navigation/index system |
| Topping name | Strong ink display/serif | Primary ledger content |
| Preview-focused row | Faint sage wash + jade leading rule/name emphasis | Identify the photograph source |
| Unselected action | Deep jade `＋ 加入` | Available action |
| Selected action | Lacquer tick; `已揀` in restrained dark lacquer | Confirm state without a full red row |
| Row separators | Muted ink at low opacity | Structure without card borders |
| Disclaimer | Muted graphite sans | Quiet operational note |

Constraints:

- Do not introduce a sixth major colour, gradient, texture or shadow.
- Gold is decorative only on this light surface; do not use low-contrast gold for numbers or instructions.
- The jade number tab is compact, square-edged and consistent. It is not a badge or rounded pill.
- The instruction rail may have a pale sage fill but no border box, radius or drop shadow.
- Preserve at least WCAG AA contrast for functional text and controls.
- Test the all-selected `10 / 10` state: ingredient names must remain visually primary and the repeated red status labels must not dominate the ledger.

### Preview alignment refinement

- Align the top of the preview panel with the ledger header or first ledger row. Do not vertically centre it several rows down.
- Use a deep-jade preview header with ivory text, or an equally high-contrast treatment using the existing palette.
- Keep the image and panel dimensions capped as already approved.
- Do not fill the remaining lower-right area with decorative copy, thumbnails or another image. The goal is to remove the accidental upper void, not to fill every space.

| Action | Selection | Preview |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Initial state | Nothing selected. | Shows `01 上湯花膠`. |
| Click/tap a row | Toggles that topping without clearing the others. | Shows that row's photograph. |
| Click/tap a selected row again | Removes only that topping. | May remain on that row. |
| Arrow keys/Home/End | Does not alter selections. | Moves preview/focus through the rows. |
| Space or Enter | Toggles the focused topping. | Keeps the focused topping in preview. |
| Hover | Does not select or change the preview. | May lighten text only. |

Use checkbox semantics (`aria-checked` or a native checkbox pattern), not single-select radio semantics and not disclosure/open state as the source of truth.

Motion:

- When motion is allowed, the preview may crossfade in under `200ms`. Opacity only.
- When `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is set, the image and name swap immediately. No crossfade, no pin, no scrub.
- Do not dispatch a fake scroll event as part of the new interaction.

## Modernity and motion plan

The current section is modern enough structurally because it uses an editorial hierarchy, asymmetric layout, restrained colour roles and direct interaction. Do not add motion merely to make the section feel designed. Finish the photographs and responsive review first.

### Phase 1 — static completion

- Keep the present paper surface, ledger proportions, typography hierarchy, action states, count placement and preview panel.
- Review all ten preview crops at desktop and mobile. Replace or reposition only the crops that do not identify the food immediately.
- The current crop audit leaves one known replacement: `/media/food/preview-fish-meat.webp` for `手打魚肉`. Replace that source with a recognisable serving that includes plate, tray or bowl context; do not redesign the panel around the weak asset.

- Apply and approve the semantic colour hierarchy and top-aligned preview treatment before any motion work.
- Do not add texture, shadows, rounded cards, background gradients or more decorative colours.

### Phase 2 — restrained interaction motion

Implement only after Phase 1 is approved:

- Preview change: opacity crossfade of approximately `140–180ms`.
- Selection change: the tick may draw or appear over approximately `100–140ms`; no bounce or scale burst.
- Hover/focus: the jade leading marker and very pale sage wash may ease in over approximately `120ms`.
- Count: update immediately. A simple colour emphasis is enough; do not animate digits through a slot-machine effect.
- All effects are local responses to user input. They must not delay selection, move surrounding layout or replay while scrolling.
- Reduced motion removes every transition and swaps state immediately.

### Phase 3 — site-wide ScrollCraft pass

Do this only after the static checklists and final media for all homepage sections are approved.

- The Hero remains the primary scrub sequence.
- The order-slip journey remains the secondary authored motion moment.
- `文記十大` must not become another pinned or scrubbed chapter. It is an interactive selection pause between those larger moments.
- A later site-wide continuity pass may add one short entrance reveal when the section first enters the viewport: heading, ledger and preview fade/translate by no more than `8–12px` over roughly `280–360ms`.
- The entrance reveal is optional and should be removed if it makes the page feel staged or slows scanning.
- Do not animate all ten rows individually, tie the preview to scroll position, or automatically change dishes while the visitor scrolls.

## Rejection conditions

Reject the implementation if any of these appear:

- A confirmed name is rewritten, shortened, translated, or hidden behind a disclosure.
- Any row is a bordered card, a filled button, or a two-column button grid.
- Selecting one topping clears another selected topping.
- Selected rows and the single preview row use the same ambiguous marker.
- Selection is indicated only by an unexplained dot or square with no `加入／已揀` text.
- An unstyled Unicode square is used as the action marker.
- The instruction is too faint to communicate that the rows can be selected.
- The selected count is aligned outside the ledger header or visually disconnected from the instruction.
- Red is used as a row background.
- A full-width black or jade background returns to this section.
- The preview is aligned with the section heading in the upper-right corner instead of with the ledger rows.
- The preview is a bare floating photograph with no context label or restrained frame.
- The preview remains vertically centred several rows below the ledger header, leaving a large accidental upper-right void.
- Header, subtitle, instruction, row numbers and names all use effectively the same visual treatment.
- Functional numbering or instructions use low-contrast gold on the paper surface.
- The preview exceeds `280px` on desktop or `180px` on mobile.
- The preview is full-bleed, pinned, or taller than the ledger on desktop.
- The section uses `100vh`, scroll-scrub, or a sticky full-screen stage.
- Hover is required to see a name or to change the preview.
- Keyboard users cannot reach and change the active row.
- A touch row is under `44px`, or the page scrolls sideways at `390px`.
- Reduced motion still animates the preview.
- Styles escape `#top-ten` and restyle the menu page.
- `OrderSlipFollow` is edited during the ledger multi-select pass.
- New generated photographs are added in order to finish the ledger.
