## Palette refinement rationale

The 2026-10-11 review recording shows a near-black `舊式香港` chapter followed by a saturated jade `HowToOrder` block and a full-black `文記十大` block. This produces a black–green–black sequence of large horizontal bands. The problem is not that jade and ink are wrong brand colours; it is that both are being used at page-sized scale.

Use a quieter surface system:

- `舊式香港` may remain the single immersive dark documentary chapter.
- `HowToOrder` moves to a very pale warm sage-paper surface.
- `文記十大` moves to the warm paper surface.
- Ink remains strong inside the ordering rail and typography; jade, gold and lacquer become accents.
- The transition should feel like turning from a night street photograph to a printed menu, not switching between coloured website bands.
