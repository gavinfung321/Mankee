# Mankee Asset Manifest and Production Plan

Status: Creative direction selected. Mankee-controlled asset collection authorised and current-site pass completed.

## Asset principles

- Real Mankee photography is the visual source of truth.
- Food, signage, interiors and people must not be fabricated when factual accuracy matters.
- AI generation is limited to approved transitions, background reconstruction or controlled experiments based on owned references.
- Every important hero state needs a desktop asset, a separately composed phone asset and a reduced-motion poster.
- Keep original masters. Delivery files are derivatives, never the only copies.
- Essential Traditional Chinese text remains semantic HTML and is never baked into generated imagery, video or 3D textures.

## Collection policy for supplied links

### Eligible after owner authorisation

- Original-size files exposed by the official Mankee website.
- Mankee-owned logo and brand artwork exposed on Mankee-controlled channels.
- Mankee-owned social photographs and videos when the original post and account are controlled by Mankee and the depicted people are cleared.
- Public operational metadata such as captions, branch references, hours and delivery destinations, subject to owner verification.

### Reference, link or embed only by default

- Hong Kong Tourism Board text and imagery.
- Michelin Guide text, photography, logos and badges.
- YouTube videos owned by broadcasters, publishers or creators.
- Reposts, tagged photographs, celebrity photographs and customer-generated content.
- Facebook or Instagram material whose original creator or licence cannot be established.

### Never infer from public visibility

Public availability does not establish commercial reuse permission. Every collected file will receive a source URL, creator/owner field, intended use, rights status and approval state. Unclear items stay outside the production asset folder.

### Web performance targets

- Initial HTML and primary copy work without WebGL.
- The hero shows a complete poster before interactive assets load.
- Keep the core GLB approximately 3–5 MB or lower after optimisation.
- Use compressed textures and geometry where visual quality permits.
- Pause rendering when the scene is offscreen.
- Cap pixel density on high-density phones.
- Provide a no-WebGL and reduced-motion composition with no empty scroll space.
- Subset approved Traditional Chinese webfonts after copy lock; do not ship complete multi-megabyte CJK font families unnecessarily.

## Rights ledger template

Every deliverable asset needs:

- Asset ID and filename.
- Creator/photographer.
- Copyright owner.
- People depicted.
- Model/property release status.
- Permitted website, advertising and social uses.
- Territory and duration restrictions.
- Third-party marks visible.
- Required credit.
- Source master location.
- Approval date and approver.
