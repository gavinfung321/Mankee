### Objects inside the one bowl

- White ceramic bowl, thin blue rings, a few small blue brush marks. Visible inner rim. Liquid below the rim.
- Dark brown broth with depth and a few irregular oil droplets. Not a flat disc and not a mirror.
- Thick yellow wheat noodles. Round in section, irregular loops, partly submerged.
- A few cooked daikon chunks with slightly translucent edges.
- One modest serving of 玻璃辣魷魚: several irregular curved squid strips with warm amber-brown glaze and darker edges, loosely grouped and partly submerged. It does not become a large tangled pile.
- Exactly two 自制韭菜餃: distinct handmade shapes with pale cooked wrappers, natural folds and restrained muted-green chive filling visible through portions of the wrapper. Both are partly submerged.
- Nothing else in the bowl. No fish balls, intestine pile, wings, mushrooms, fish maw, pork knuckle, egg, garnish heap, or a second noodle type.
