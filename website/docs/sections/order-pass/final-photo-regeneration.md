## Consolidated corrections

### 1. Spoon — required

- Increase the entire Chinese soup spoon by about **20%**.
- Make the scoop visibly wider and deeper, with a slightly thicker handle.
- Keep it seated in the broth and supported by the bowl; it must not float.
- Keep the handle pointing toward the lower left and stopping before the HTML-slip reserve.
- Change the current bright orange toward a muted amber-orange plastic.

### 2. Bowl fill and broth — required

- Make the bowl feel slightly less crowded: reduce visible noodle volume by about **10–15%**.
- Keep the liquid clearly below the inner rim.
- Reveal a little more open dark broth around the ingredients and spoon.
- Keep reflections irregular and restrained; no mirror surface or flat dark disc.
- Preserve enough visible broth for a later tiny highlight shimmer.

### 3. Squid identity — required

- Reduce the squid pile by about **10–15%** without removing it.
- Replace the most tubular, intestine-like forms with irregular flattened and gently curled squid strips.
- Use varied strip widths, subtle translucent amber edges, darker braised edges and natural cuts.
- Avoid tentacles, worms, noodles, intestine anatomy and repeated identical pieces.

### 4. Dumplings — required

- Keep **exactly two** dumplings.
- Make their silhouettes visibly different and handmade rather than duplicated.
- Improve wrapper folds, soft cooked translucency and restrained muted-green chive visibility.
- Keep both partly submerged and integrated with broth; no fluorescent green and no waxy plastic surface.

### 5. Daikon and noodles — realism refinement

- Keep the existing daikon role but make the pieces slightly less block-perfect, with cooked translucent edges and small natural surface variation.
- Keep thick yellow wheat noodles, but introduce modest variation in thickness and curvature.
- Reduce uniform gloss and repeated parallel loops; keep some noodles naturally submerged.

### 6. Chopsticks — realism refinement

- Preserve one complete parallel pair and the same general placement.
- Reduce the lacquered/plastic shine and use natural matte wood grain.
- Clarify physical contact with the bowl rim.
- Both tips and both handle ends remain visible; no fused, floating or extra stick.

### 7. Condiments — preserve scale, improve staging

- Keep the current enlarged scale; do not make the containers smaller again.
- Keep exactly four containers representing 麻油、椒粉、豉油、脆蒜.
- Loosen the line-up slightly with small, believable depth offsets so it does not resemble a product catalogue.
- Preserve all caps inside the frame and keep the group behind, not overlapping, the bowl.
- Improve glass thickness, contents, cap materials and contact shadows.
- No logos, readable text or commercial branding.

### 8. Wood, colour and photographic finish — required

- Reduce the orange saturation and metallic bronze appearance of the counter.
- Make it dark warm brown wood with irregular grain, ordinary scratches and a mostly matte finish.
- Reduce repetitive rippling texture, excessive clarity and global sharpening.
- Keep the left slip reserve visually quieter than the bowl area so HTML remains legible.
- Preserve the warm upper-left direction but use more natural restaurant colour balance and softer highlight roll-off.
- The result must read as a real restaurant photograph, not HDR, CGI or AI-polished advertising.
