# Photographic generation standards

Status: **mandatory for every future generated or edited photograph in the Mankee project.**

## Permanent synthetic-texture ban

No accepted image may contain repetitive embossed, rippled, tessellated, stamped, engraved, letter-like, geometric or watermark-like microtexture.

This ban applies to every material and every area of the frame, including:

- wood, laminate, stone, tile and counters;
- food, broth, sauces and steam;
- ceramic, glass, metal, plastic and fabric;
- skin, shadows, reflections and background blur.

The same artificial texture must never appear across unrelated materials. A result that looks acceptable at thumbnail size still fails if repetition is visible at 100%.

## Mandatory review procedure

Every generated photographic candidate must be inspected at 100% before approval.

- Scan the entire frame, not only the requested edit.
- Inspect background, neutral surfaces, highlights and shadows separately.
- Reject repeated squares, polygons, scales, loops, grooves, cross-hatching, ripples, symbols or shared synthetic grain.
- Compare wood and material texture against a real photographic donor when one is available.
- Do not preserve an inherited synthetic texture as a locked invariant.
- Do not hide the defect using blur, compression, darkness, depth of field or an overlay.
- If a global synthetic texture exists, clean the photographic foundation before making further local food edits or animation.

## Prompt requirement

Every future photographic generation or edit prompt must include this negative requirement:

> No repetitive embossed, rippled, tessellated, stamped, engraved, geometric, letter-like or shared synthetic microtexture anywhere in the image. All materials must have distinct, irregular, physically plausible photographic texture.

## Approval rule

The synthetic-texture ban is a hard gate. Any violation rejects the whole candidate. It cannot be deferred as a minor polish item.
